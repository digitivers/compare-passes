Questions travellers ask

Istanbul pass FAQ

Which Istanbul pass is best?

It depends on how many paid sights you will visit. For three or more a day over 1 to 7 days, Istanbul Tourist Pass covers all 6 landmarks, Bosphorus cruises and guided tours. For one or two sights a day, Flexi Pass or single tickets cost less.

Is Istanbul Tourist Pass licensed?

Yes. It is sold by Cityberry Tourism Agency, a TÜRSAB member with licence number 11745, under Turkish law.

Why does Istanbul Tourist Pass cost more than Other Pass?

On a 3-day trip the difference is about €8 a day. It pays for a TÜRSAB-licensed Turkish seller, 20+ attractions and experiences the other pass does not list, and audio guides in 25 languages.

Can I buy a 4-day or 6-day Istanbul pass?

Yes. Istanbul Tourist Pass is sold for every length from 1 to 7 days, including 4 and 6 days.

Which Istanbul pass is best for families?

Istanbul Tourist Pass has child rates for ages 5 to 12 and includes family activities such as Flyzone Air Sports, aquariums and theme parks.

Does an Istanbul pass let me skip security lines?

No pass can. You skip the ticket line, and security checks still apply at every major site.

Which languages are the audio guides in?

Most major sites have an audio guide recorded in 25 languages. You can download it over wifi and listen with no signal.

How long is Istanbul Tourist Pass valid?

Up to one year from purchase. The pass activates when you first use it, and each day runs to midnight rather than for 24 hours.
